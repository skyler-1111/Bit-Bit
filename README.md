Bit-Bit
----------------------
A neo-brutalist virtual pet + productivity companion.

Bit-Bit is a browser-based virtual pet inspired by classic digital pets, rebuilt with a neo-brutalist visual style and a few extra systems: a Pomodoro-style focus timer, coin economy, customization shop, themes, music, and system logs.

Take care of Bit-Bit or it dies 🥀 🫩

**Features**

- Virtual Pet
Keep Bit-Bit alive by managing three needs:
🍎 Hunger — decreases over time and can be restored by feeding Bit-Bit.
🧼 Hygiene — gradually decreases and can be restored by cleaning.
❤️ Social — decreases the fastest and requires regular interaction (you gotta pet it).

If Bit-Bit's social level reaches zero, the system goes RIP.

- Interactions
1. Feed Bit-Bit
2. Clean Bit-Bit
3. Put Bit-Bit to sleep
4. Pet Bit-Bit to increase its social level
5. Watch Bit-Bit's appearance change depending on its condition

- Shop
Earn coins through the focus timer and spend them on:

1. Energy Drinks
2. Luxury Soap
3. Auto-Petter (most useful one)
4. Custom shades
5. Different Bit-Bit skin colors

**Bit-Bit also doubles as a productivity companion.**

- 25-minute Focus Mode
- 5-minute Break Mode
- Start / pause / reset controls
- Earn coins while the timer runs
- Audio notification when the timer finishes
